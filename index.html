Four roles, one platform

Students
See your groups and lessons, answer questions, book a mentor, earn coins and spend them in the shop.
Teachers
Write questions for each lesson, record attendance for the day's class and review student answers.
Mentors
Set your working hours, support assigned groups and run one-to-one help sessions.
Administrators
Manage users, courses, groups, rooms, schedules, news and shop stock from a single admin area.

What you can run with EdTrack

Courses and lessons

Build a course with a length in months, split it into ordered modules, and fill each module with lessons.

Groups and rooms

Put a teacher, a mentor and a list of students into a group, and give every room a capacity.

Weekly schedules

Set the weekday, time and room for each group, then generate its lessons from the start date.

Attendance

Mark each student present, late or absent. The group page shows a full student-by-date grid.

Written assessment

Students answer lesson questions. Teachers or mentors give a score and feedback, and students see the result.

Mentor sessions

Mentors publish 40-minute slots. Students pick a topic from a lesson and book a time that fits.

Show up, answer well, earn coins.

Every coin a student earns or spends is written to a ledger, so the balance always adds up. Rankings show who is leading, and the shop turns effort into real rewards.

  • 3coins for being present
  • 1coin for arriving late
  • 0coins for an absence

Shop items have a price and limited stock. A purchase takes coins and reduces stock in one step, so two students can never buy the last item.

From empty center to first lesson

  1. Set up the course

    Create the course, its modules and lessons, and add rooms.

  2. Form a group

    Choose a teacher and mentor, set a start date and enroll students.

  3. Plan the week

    Add weekly times and generate the group's lessons.

  4. Teach and track

    Record attendance, collect answers and give feedback.

  5. Reward progress

    Coins flow to students, who spend them in the shop.

News from your center

Post announcements with a title, text and an image, and publish them on the home page so every student sees them first.
