Turn participation into motivation.

EdTrack's coin system rewards students for being active in their learning journey. Attendance contributes to their balance, rankings show progress and the shop turns earned coins into real rewards.

  • 3 coins for being present
  • 1 coin for arriving late
  • 0 coins for an absence

Every transaction is recorded in a ledger, keeping student balances consistent while shop inventory remains controlled.
